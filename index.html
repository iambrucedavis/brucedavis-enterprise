Personal technical work

Check what is missing before work starts.

I built three personal, AI-assisted prototypes to check sample plans and designs. They use synthetic data, not customer data, and are separate from my employer work.

PAM Onboarding Readiness

Which accounts are ready for the next rollout group?

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Checks whether sample accounts have owners, completed tests, and approval flags before proposing rollout groups. It uses supplied information and does not change accounts.

My contribution: I defined what to check and built the rules, sample account list, report, and tests with AI assistance.

Keep in mind: The tool trusts the sample account information. People still need to check the findings and approve any rollout.

Try this: choose “Blocked” to see what each account is missing.

Infrastructure Design Validator

What needs attention in this design?

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Checks a sample design for selected network, dependency, ownership, and recovery issues. It does not inspect live cloud systems or certify an architecture.

My contribution: I defined the checks and built the sample design, validation rules, system diagram, report, and tests with AI assistance.

Keep in mind: A technical lead still needs to check the supplied design and its assumptions.

Change Readiness Tracker

What is missing from this change plan?

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Highlights missing prerequisites and scheduling conflicts, then compares two versions of a change plan. Being ready for review does not mean a change is approved to execute.

My contribution: I defined the checks and built the sample plans, comparison rules, report, and tests with AI assistance.

Keep in mind: The tool does not verify the supplied records or carry out changes.
