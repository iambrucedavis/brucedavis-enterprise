Scope and context
  • The readiness checks use supplied account fields. They do not verify whether the work was completed.
  • Matching dependency_group values identify accounts that need to stay together. The tool does not discover these connections.
  • The group limit counts accounts. It does not estimate effort or dates.
  • Each rollout group needs human review, a confirmed time window, and approval before proceeding. This report does not change accounts.

Technical status values and codes

“Proposed wave” means a proposed rollout group. “Dependency hold” means waiting on related accounts. “Capacity review” means a related-account group exceeds the account-count limit.
