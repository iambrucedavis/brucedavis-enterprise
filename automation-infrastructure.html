PAM Onboarding Readiness

What is blocking the next account-onboarding wave?

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Checks supplied ownership, vault onboarding, rotation and rollback testing, and change-approval flags. Uses declared dependencies to propose rollout waves.

My contribution: Defined the readiness requirements and acceptance criteria; developed the rule checks, sample inventory, report, and tests with AI assistance.

Important limitations: Dependencies and readiness flags are supplied, not discovered or independently verified. Proposed waves still need technical review and approval.

Requirements, implementation, and limitations

Checks five supplied flags: accountable owner, vault onboarding, rotation testing, rollback testing, and change approval. Keeps supplied dependency groups together; a blocked member holds its eligible peers. Sample output: 12 accounts, 6 in 3 proposed waves, 4 blocked, and 2 dependency holds. The optional AI adapter was tested with simulated responses; live AI calls and output quality remain unverified.

Dependencies come from the supplied inventory. The tool does not discover them or verify approval evidence.

Infrastructure Design Validator

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Change Readiness Tracker

What is preventing this change from being ready for review?

Personal project · AI-assisted · Synthetic data · Read-only

Checks supplied change windows, resource conflicts, prerequisites, approvals, testing, and rollback readiness, then compares two plan snapshots.

My contribution: Defined readiness requirements and sample plans; developed the comparison logic, findings report, and tests with AI assistance.

Important limitations: Evidence references and prerequisite completion are unverified. Ready for review is not approval to execute; no changes are executed.

View example report → View code and tests ↗
Requirements, implementation, and limitations

Checks supplied windows, exclusive-resource conflicts, prerequisites, approval flags, testing, rollback readiness, and evidence references. Compares two plan snapshots. Four synthetic changes become ready for review after sample corrections; the tool does not verify evidence or execute changes.

Ready for review is not approval to execute. Resource and evidence assumptions still require human review.

Recorded validation: 14 automated tests passed on November 2, 2026.

Limits that apply to all three

  • No real accounts or cloud resources are changed.
  • Readiness findings are not approval to execute.
  • Test results and demonstration counts are not customer outcomes.
  • Only simulated AI responses were tested; optional live AI functionality has not been verified.

Python 3.9+ and the standard library are sufficient for the documented local execution path. The recorded 50-test validation used python3 run_all.py with synthetic scenarios.
