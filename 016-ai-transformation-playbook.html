Personal framework

A framework for evaluating AI initiatives

A personal framework for assessing business value, implementation risk, governance needs, and the next practical step for an AI initiative.

Conceptual planning framework · Not evidence of a delivered transformation program

Start with the business question

What problem needs solving, who experiences it, and what evidence would show that a proposed solution helps? Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria before choosing a model or platform.

Check implementation risk

Identify dependencies, data boundaries, technical constraints, testing needs, and recovery options. A useful demonstration can surface questions; it does not establish production readiness.

Make governance needs explicit

Identify accountable owners and where human approval is needed before an action proceeds. Decide what information can be used and what evidence a reviewer needs.

Choose the next practical step

A proposed next step could be a requirements review, a bounded prototype, a pilot proposal, or a decision not to proceed. Any scoring criteria, weights, timelines, and success thresholds would need agreement for that context.

Optional evaluation prompts
  • Business value: named problem, intended users, measurable acceptance criteria, and alternatives.
  • Feasibility: data access, integration effort, dependencies, operating cost assumptions, and testing.
  • Governance: ownership, permissions, information handling, review points, and recovery.
  • Pilot scope: boundaries, evidence needed, exit criteria, and a human decision before wider use.

These are proposed evaluation prompts, not a record of employer decisions or an independently validated scoring method.
